## Design notes against the TS

- **D1** The stock C-beams are 480 mm apart (inner faces) in `Tevo Black Widow assembly.step`, not 500. The 20x80x500 top tie sits on the C-beam ends and does not fit between them; it is cut to 480. WP0 confirms.
- **D2** In the project model the 40x80x550 extrusion is the bed (Y) axis beam; the stock X gantry is two 20x40x485 bars. This model uses the 40x80x550 as the Y bridge (TS 4 item 2) and the 20x40 pair, cut to 480, as the second tie (TS 7.1).
- **D3** The bridge sits on 32 mm risers so the beam underside (Z 153) clears the tape bank, magazine and clamps. The head hangs in front of the beam (+X), so the nozzle reach is offset by 65.0 mm in X.
- **D4** The X belts run inside the open C channels; the C-beam end caps are replaced by printed drive and idler blocks. The jackshaft sits at the left end (TS 7.1).
- **D5** Head Z motor defaults to NEMA8_LIN30 to cut mass; the stock NEMA17 is the fallback named in WP4 step 6.
- **D6 (rev B)** The 20x80x210 legs are gone. Four printed corner blocks are end cap, pulley/idler housing, jackshaft bearing seat (608ZZ pressed in), tie corner bracket and leg in one; the front-left one also holds the X motor. Rubber feet screw into them.
- **D7 (rev B)** Tape lanes, up camera and nozzle magazine sit in a strip right of the bridge travel (X > 526, beam right face at most X 522), so the beam never passes over them. Tapes feed in from the right end. Magazine pockets run along Y (TS 7.5 says along X; the change is still single-axis motion).
- **D8 (rev B)** One tooling plate carries the PCB fixture and the whole tooling strip, so all feeder, camera and magazine coordinates share one datum. 3-point support: left tie + two posts on the right tie.
- **D9 (rev B)** Head = one printed head carriage (mother plate, head plate, Z motor bracket, camera bracket, switch arm, belt clamp, chain post) + one machined Z slide (Z plate, both bearing bores in one setup for FR-14, nut seat, C motor shelf). Bridge = one printed end per side (foot plate, riser, belt clamp, chain arm). The LP mother plate is no longer used.
- **D10 (rev B)** Vacuum panel and electronics box are not modelled for now; TS 7.4 and 8 still apply.
- **D11 (rev D, current head)** The head uses a NEMA8 hollow-shaft C motor and a NEMA8 external linear Z motor with integral T5x2 screw. The nozzle stack is approximately 15 g; bearing runout and loaded repeatability must be measured on the selected motor. Weigh the assembled head and verify motor torque at the actual supply voltage before setting acceleration.
- **D12 (superseded feeder history)** The v2.0/v2.1 lane used a flexure pawl and ratchet-based peel mechanism and is archived under `legacy/feeder_v2.1/`. It is not the current geometry. Current feeder details are D14 and `feeder/ASSEMBLY.md`.
- **D13 (rev F, dynamics)** (1) Aluminium profiles now carry catalogue kg/m instead of the solid-core model volume: the bridge beam was 3.6 kg in the model, ~1.3 kg in reality, so X moves 3.08 kg, not ~5.3 kg. (2) Z runs at 1/4 microstepping (400 steps/mm, 2.5 um): at 1/16 PR-3 needs 96k steps/s, over the ~40k of the 8-bit board. Configure the Z driver jumpers and Marlin DEFAULT_AXIS_STEPS_PER_UNIT to match. (3) OpenPnP Safe Z = -9.2 (tip 2 mm over the tallest tooling in reach, the magazine tips); travel at Safe Z is checked in `pnp check`. Together they bring the PR-10 estimate from ~610 to ~724 parts/h. (4) The torque checks assume a 24 V supply; at 12 V the X margin drops to ~1.9 (WP0 confirms).
- **D14 (current v3.0 feeder)** Eight independent servo lanes use a rigid spring-loaded feed pawl and detent, plus a rack/idler/pinion peel drive with two HF0612 one-way bearings per lane. The segmented cover plate is screw-locked; lane body and side plate split so PETG parts print without supports. One separate Nano controller runs the servos. This geometry is awaiting its documented physical first article; see `feeder/README.md` and `feeder/TEST.md`.
